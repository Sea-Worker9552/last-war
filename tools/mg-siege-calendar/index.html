⚔️ Alliance Execution Protocol

1. Server Reset Timing

Daily reset is at 00:00:00 SVT. Events do not start immediately at reset to give members 10 minutes to log in, clear queues, and set radar.

2. Joint Day Priority

On joint days, Marshal's Guard begins at 00:10 SVT. The defense/rally period lasts ~15-20 min. Zombie Siege is launched at 00:50 SVT (40 min after MG begins).

3. Weekend & Buster Day Reductions

Attendance is split during weekend nights and stamina is preserved for Buster Day. Base level automatically steps down by 1.

4. Buster Day (Saturday) Rescheduling

Enemy Buster opens at reset (00:00 SVT) with aggressive cross-server PvP raids. Saturday events are shifted to 21:00 SVT (3 hours before next reset) once Buster fighting calms down, so alliance defense and rally attendance remain strong.
