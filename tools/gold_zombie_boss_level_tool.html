🔒 HOW TO KEEP YOUR LEVEL FROM INCREASING (LEVEL FREEZE TACTICS)

Since boss level selection is random within your spawning range, here is how commanders prevent accidental level increases when high-level bosses appear.

⏳ 1. Let High-End Random Spawns Expire

Boss summons pick a random level from your range (e.g. Lvl 15~35 at Lvl 30). If the top Lvl 35 boss spawns, DO NOT KILL IT. Let the timer run out! Rule 1 requires the boss to be killed to update your level.

👥 2. Join Rallies as Member (Never Lead)

If an alliance member or sniped enemy spawns a high-level boss, join as a regular squad member instead of starting the rally. Rule 2 (+5) ONLY triggers if you are the Rally Leader!

🛡️ 3. Only Kill Spawns ≤ Nominal Level

Only rally and defeat spawned bosses at or below your nominal level (e.g. Lvl 15, 20, 25, 30). Killing these will grant full event points without triggering a level increase!

🎖️ COMMANDER'S SUMMARY CHEAT SHEET

01

Accelerated Level Escalation

Summoning & leading your own boss triggers Rule 1 (sets to Boss Level) + Rule 2 (+5 boost), pushing your level to Boss Level + 5. Beware: this moves your zombie level up faster than anything else!

02

Fixed 20-Level Spawning Window

Your spawning range is always a fixed 20-level span ([Level - 15] to [Level + 5]). Leveling up does not widen your range—it simply shifts your 20-level window upwards to harder bosses.

03

How to Prevent Level Increases

If a boss higher than your level spawns randomly, do NOT kill it (let it expire unkilled). When joining rallies on higher bosses, join as a member (never lead) to prevent Rule 2 from triggering.

04

Permanent Progress (Never Decreases)

Your Summoning Level is permanent across all zombie events and can only increase, never drop back down. Every level gained locks in your new higher spawning range forever!
