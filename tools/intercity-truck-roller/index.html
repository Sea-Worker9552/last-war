📋 Official Game Instructions

[Probability Details]

1. Different rarity Trucks have varying refresh weights based on refresh counts (including free refreshes):

  • (1): N(50) R(30) SR(10) SSR(5) UR(5)
  • (2): N(30) R(30) SR(20) SSR(10) UR(10)
  • (3): N(20) R(20) SR(30) SSR(15) UR(15)
  • (4): N(0) R(20) SR(40) SSR(20) UR(20)
  • (5): N(0) R(0) SR(50) SSR(25) UR(25)
  • (>5): N(0) R(0) SR(0) SSR(0) UR(100)

[Reindeer Sleigh Ride]

2. After unlocking the Reindeer Sleigh Ride, it has 10 fixed chances in Truck refreshes with Trade Contracts, independent of refresh frequency.

3. Probability calculation: If the Truck weight is UR(100), Reindeer Sleigh Ride(10), the chance for a UR truck is 90.9% (100/110), and for a Reindeer Sleigh Ride is 9.1% (10/110).

[Cost & Dispatch Rules]

• Initial truck is randomized automatically upon spawning (weights: 50% N, 30% R, 10% SR, 5% SSR, 5% UR).

• 1 Free Refresh is granted on each truck lane.

• Subsequent refreshes cost 1 Trade Contract (or 100 Diamonds) each.

• Roll 6 triggers the pity guarantee (100% UR, or 90.9% UR / 9.1% Sleigh if researched).
